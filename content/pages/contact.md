# Contact

For anything to do with this site — a correction, a question about a guide, a
bug in one of the tools, or a suggestion for something worth building — the
address is:

**info@julienika.cz**

## What is worth writing about

**Corrections.** These are the most useful messages to receive. If a guide
states something incorrectly, or a calculator produces a result you can show is
wrong, please say which page and what the correct figure or behaviour should
be. Fixes are made promptly and the page is updated.

**Bugs.** If a tool misbehaves, it helps enormously to know which browser you
were using and what you entered. Several of the tools do their work inside your
browser rather than on a server, so behaviour occasionally differs between
browsers in ways that are hard to reproduce blind.

**Suggestions.** If there is a calculation you keep doing by hand, it may be
worth building. No promises, but the list of tools here grew from exactly that
kind of suggestion.

## What this site cannot help with

It cannot give individual professional advice. The guides explain general
principles, and questions like whether a specific recipe is safe, whether a
particular kiln schedule suits your clay, or whether a given fish belongs in
your tank depend on specifics only you can see. Where something carries real
risk — caustic chemicals, high temperatures, live animals — please rely on the
manufacturer's data sheet, a local expert, or the relevant regulations rather
than a general reference site.

## Response time

This is a personal project run alongside other work, so replies are not
instant. Corrections and bug reports are prioritised over everything else.
